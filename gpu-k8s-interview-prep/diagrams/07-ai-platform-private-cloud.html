Private-cloud GPU/AI platform: the layers

What you would lead, and where the risks sit

NOT: A pile of GPU servers with a Kubernetes cluster on top.
IS: A product: self-service experience, platform services, event backbone, GPU layer and foundation, run to SLOs and billed per tenant.

  1. A private-cloud GPU/AI platform, top to bottom. Each layer has an owner and an SLO.🛺 Shopping mall: shopfront (portal), management office (services), intercom (Kafka), plumbing (K8s/GPU), foundations, land.
  2. Experience: tenants self-serve through a portal, APIs and CLI (SPA frontend, contract-first APIs).
  3. Platform services (Java/Spring Boot): tenants, quotas, catalogue, provisioning orchestration, metering, inference gateway. This is where your teams build.
  4. Kafka carries lifecycle, usage and audit events so services stay decoupled and billing can replay.
  5. Kubernetes + GPU layer: Operator, MIG or DRA, Kueue for fair share, DCGM for health and cost, model servers.
  6. Cloud foundation: bare-metal provisioning, identity, networking, storage, secrets.
  7. Infrastructure: GPU servers on a fast fabric. Capacity is scarce and has long lead times.
  8. Cross-cutting: security (isolation, audit, CVEs), SRE (SLOs, incidents), FinOps (cost per tenant). A director owns how these three meet delivery.
