Spring Boot gateway in front of GPU inference

Protect the GPU, scale on the queue, add nodes on demand

NOT: Autoscaling on CPU or GPU utilisation alone. A busy GPU may still have batch headroom, and CPU says nothing about the queue.
IS: Bulkhead and timeouts at the gateway, queue-depth scaling with KEDA, node scaling with Karpenter.

  1. Spring Boot gateway in front of vLLM, and how the platform scales under load.🛺 Reception desk (gateway) in front of a busy kitchen (GPU): limit the orders in, grow the kitchen when the queue builds.
  2. Clients reach the gateway through the ingress.
  3. Each gateway replica caps in-flight calls (bulkhead). Over the cap → 429 + Retry-After instead of overloading the GPU.
  4. Admitted requests are forwarded to the vLLM Service on GPU nodes.
  5. Demand rises: vLLM's waiting queue grows. This is the right scaling signal, not raw GPU utilisation.
  6. KEDA reads the queue from Prometheus and raises vLLM replicas 2 → 4.
  7. New Pods are Pending (no free GPUs) so Karpenter launches a GPU node. Image pull and model load take minutes, so keep a warm floor.
  8. New replicas pass their startupProbe and readinessProbe, the queue drains, and the 429s stop.
